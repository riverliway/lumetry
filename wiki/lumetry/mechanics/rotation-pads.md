# rotation-pads

[Prof. Radian](#lumetry/characters/prof-radian.md)'s hardware. A pad sits under a block; when [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) uses it, the block on top spins one hex step in place, a sixth of a turn, without leaving its cell.

Note that a rotation pad can never change a mirror's actual form of short (60deg) vs long (120deg) reflection.
