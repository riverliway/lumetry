# pushable-mirrors

[Prof. Newton](#lumetry/characters/prof-newton.md)'s hardware. A mirror mounted on a track: [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) walks into it and shoves it along the track to reposition where the beam bounces. She cannot push it off the track, into another block, or out of the room, which is what makes placement a puzzle rather than free rearrangement.

A mirror turns a beam by a hex step rather than a right angle, so routing light means thinking in sixths of a circle. Two mirror shapes give two different bounces: a short mirror aligned to a cell edge, and a long one aligned corner to corner, each sending an incoming beam a different way.
