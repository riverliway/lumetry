# detectors

The measurement devices, the goal of every room. Guide a beam into each device and, once all of them read, the chamber door unlocks and the next [experiment](#lumetry/gameplay/overview.md) opens.

At their simplest a device just wants light. [Prof. Lumen](#lumetry/characters/prof-lumen.md) complicates them: some grow picky about which [color](#lumetry/mechanics/prisms.md) satisfies them, and some stop being pure goals and start *doing* things when lit, opening doors, spinning a [rotation pad](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md).
