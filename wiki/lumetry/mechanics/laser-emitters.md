# laser-emitters

The source of every beam. An emitter sits on the hex grid and throws a straight line of white light in the direction it faces, running until the light hits something or runs out of [reach](#lumetry/mechanics/beam-length.md).

Emitters can be toggled on and off, and they face a direction like any other placed block, so a [rotation pad](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md) under one becomes a way to re-aim an entire puzzle. Live beams are solid: [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) cannot walk through her own light, which turns the beam itself into a movable wall.

Present from the first room. Everything else in the game is a way of bending, splitting, shortening, or concentrating what the emitter produces.
