# cooperation

[Lux](#lumetry/characters/lux.md)'s "mechanic," and the one that breaks the faculty pattern. Every professor before this hands [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) a machine and steps back. Lux hands her nothing. By the final wing Tess has been the student in every room; now she is the one teaching, and Lux is the intern she has to walk through the lab in one piece.

## The inversion

Lux is [illuminance](#lumetry/characters/lux.md) made a person: the unit for light landing *on* a surface, not light a source throws off. The mechanic takes that literally. Every [detector](#lumetry/mechanics/detectors.md) so far has been a fixed box you route a beam into. Lux is a mobile, living detector, and getting the right light to land on them is what the room wants. It closes the whole game: you spent seventeen rooms walking light to a goal, and now the goal walks, badly, and you have to walk it.

## Two kinds of body

- **Tess is opaque.** Live beams have always been solid to her; her body casts a shadow and blocks light.
- **Lux is a receiver.** Light is supposed to land on Lux, but Lux is [timid](#lumetry/characters/lux.md) to the point of paralysis: won't step into a live beam, freezes near [Joule](#lumetry/characters/prof-joule.md)'s destructive light, balks at a color they don't trust.

So the tension is built from opposites. Tess casts shadow, Lux needs light. You spend the finale arranging the one to shelter and deliver the other: clear a safe lane, park Tess's body as a moving wall so a beam doesn't send Lux bolting, then coax them onto the exact cell the light has to reach.

## Directing Lux

Three ways to run the control, roughly cheapest to richest. This is the open design lever:

- **Follow-and-place.** Lux trails Tess; a `use` toward Lux plants them where they stand or calls them onward. Simplest to build, one stick of control, reads instantly as "leading someone."
- **Swap control.** Toggle which body you drive, *Lost Vikings* / *Trine* style. More expressive, doubles the input surface, wants its own teaching room.
- **Point-and-coax.** Mark a target cell and Lux picks their own nervous path there, refusing any step that's illegal or frightening, so the pathing itself becomes the puzzle.

The lean-scope pick is follow-and-place, with swap-control held in reserve if the finale wants two beams live at once.

## The finale

The old plan had Lux cause an accident that set off a mega-puzzle. Cut. The finale is the cooperation wing itself, [Rooms 16 to 18](#lumetry/rooms/room-16.md): a gentle first room where Lux is introduced and the leading is easy, a middle room that leans on shielding and timing, and the last [experiment](#lumetry/gameplay/overview.md), which cashes in every mechanic at once with a second, frightened person in the room. Clearing it earns [Passing the Torch](#lumetry/gameplay/achievements.md). Tess turning teacher, and finally earning her unit (Tess, short for Tesla), is the beat the entire run has been quietly building toward.
