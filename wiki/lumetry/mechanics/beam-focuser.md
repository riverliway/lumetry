# beam-focuser

[Prof. Joule](#lumetry/characters/prof-joule.md)'s hardware, and the opposite of the [prism](#lumetry/mechanics/prisms.md). Where a prism scatters one beam into three, the focuser collects three beams and concentrates them into a single destructive beam.

That focused beam is the only light in the game that destroys. It melts certain blocks, clearing walls or hardware no ordinary beam can touch. It arrives late, once the player is fluent enough to feed three beams into one mouth at the same time.
