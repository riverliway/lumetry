# prisms

[Prof. Lumen](#lumetry/characters/prof-lumen.md)'s hardware. A prism takes an incoming white beam and splits it into a fan of colored beams, currently cyan, magenta, and yellow, spraying out in different directions from the split. One beam becomes three, each going its own way.

Colored light is a one-way street through a prism: a beam that is already colored gets absorbed rather than re-split, so the player can't chain prisms to infitely split a beam. The point of color is the [detectors](#lumetry/mechanics/detectors.md) that only accept a specific one, which turns a single split into a routing problem across three simultaneous beams.
