# Prof. Lumen (she/her)

Puts glitter on everything. Think Mabel from *Gravity Falls* with lab access. She introduces [prisms](#lumetry/mechanics/prisms.md), which split white light into colors, and expands the [measurement devices](#lumetry/mechanics/detectors.md) into things that do more than open a door. She is married to Prof. Candela.

![Concept sketch of Prof. Lumen](/lumetry/characters/prof-lumen.png)

## Unit and shape

The lumen measures luminous flux, the total light a source pours out in every direction at once. That is glitter as a physical quantity: light going everywhere. Her arrival is where the game turns from one white beam into a spread of colored ones. Brightest, most saturated design in the cast, per [art direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md).

## Art

Large, wild, curly hair that is dark towards the roots and has a pink ombre. Shining beads woven into her strands. Has a lush, chocolately skin color. Very curvy. Wears transparent yellow and pink glasses. Has a pan flag skirt under her lab coat.
