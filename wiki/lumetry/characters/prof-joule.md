# Prof. Joule (he/him)

An explosive personality. Thrilled about everything, wired with energy, and a little crazy. Think Dr. Doofinshmirtz from Phineas and Ferb. He introduces [the beam focuser](#lumetry/mechanics/beam-focuser.md).

## Unit and shape

The joule measures energy, and his mechanic is energy concentrated to the point of violence: three beams collected and focused into one destructive beam that melts certain blocks. He is the late-game escalation, the point where light stops being gentle. Loud, high-contrast design, per [art direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md).

## Art

Vertical scar over one eye (that eye is always closed). Deep olive, mediterannian-greek skin. Salt and pepper hair. Has tubes pumping fluid into his midriff (is it drugs? nitroglycern? no. it is insulin for his diabetes.) Looks and acts like a villan at all times, even though he is actually a pretty nice guy if you get to know him.

Ace flag embedded in the design/colors somewhere.

## Dialogue

Crazed, explosive speech. Tosses random greek words into his speech when he is excited/surprised (which is always).
