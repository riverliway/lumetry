# Prof. Newton (he/him)

The himboiest man you have ever seen. Absolutely jacked, heart of pure gold, roughly Kronk with a doctorate in experimental physics. He introduces [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) to [pushable mirrors on tracks](#lumetry/mechanics/pushable-mirrors.md).

![Concept sketch of Prof. Newton](/lumetry/characters/prof-newton.png)

## Unit and shape

The newton is the SI unit of force, which is the whole joke: his mechanic is the one solved by shoving. His [art-direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md) shape leans into mass and muscle, big and solid, and he likely anchors an early room so pushing is the first verb the player learns past walking. *(Speculative: pairing the strongest-looking professor with the most physical mechanic is the cleanest read.)*

## Art

Newton is a thickly built man with a massive chest. His chest has patches of hair, but is otherwise smooth. Tanned, hawaiian-esque. He is wearing a while lab coat that is singed at the bottom edges. His right arm is mechanical and his left arm is flesh. His mechanical arm has the colors of the MLM flag. He has a beard going up to his sideburns, but no mustache. Thick eyebrows, and wears darkened goggles on his forehead at all times.

### Sprites

**Welcoming** - Right hand extended palm facing up. Left hand on his hip/waist. Goggles on his forehead, warm facial expression.

**Happy** - Both hands on hip/waist. Head slightly back and chest puffed out. Huge smile

**Thinking** - Right hand stroking his beard. Left hand supporting his right elbow.

**Satisfied** - Arms crossed. Leaning back, relaxed.

**Grimacing** - Right arm covers his face, but the fingers are split so he is still peaking out of them. Mouth is showing teeth together. Head/chest tilted aways but eyes still looking back towards Tess.

## Dialogue

Newton is a kind, enthusiastic, empathetic teacher. He likes to lift people up and see them succeed. He takes critisism, sarcasm, and insults with stride. He is a workaholic and likes to push people to new heights.

Think Kronk from Emperor's New Groove, Prof. Kukui from Pokemon Sun & Moon, and Kineshi Hairo from Saiki K.

Tess tries to match his enthusiasm, even during the painful moments.
