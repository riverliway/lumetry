# Prof. Radian (they/them)

Androgynous and gloriously flamboyant. Half their dialogue portraits catch them mid-ballet. They can and WILL strike a JoJo pose without provocation. They introduce [rotation pads](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md).

![Concept sketch of Prof. Radian](/lumetry/characters/prof-radian.png)

## Unit and shape

The radian measures angle, which is exactly what their hardware changes: a rotation pad turns a block in place. The posing is the joke made visual, a person who communicates in angles. Their [art-direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md) shape should read as line and pose over bulk. *(introduces them right after [Newton](#lumetry/characters/prof-newton.md) so the player learns to change orientation once they can already change position.)*

## Art

Radian is a dynamic, fabolous figure. They are slim waisted, sharp chin, and a ponytail. A healthy, rosey peach colored skin. They have two scarfs of purple and yellow (enby flag), black hair, and a white lab coat. They are constantly in ridicouls jojos style pozes and obsessed with dance. They often have their head tilted up, not making eye contact with the player.

### Level Decor

* Ballet shoes

### Sprites

**Welcoming** - clutching their scarves looking away from the player

**Grimacing** - Standing on one foot, fully bent over backwards, other knee out, hand over forehead, fainting

**Satisifed** - Facing away from Tess, one leg resting on the edge of the screen, bent over backwards to face the player, palms out to express joy

## Dialogue 

Radian is overly dramatic, narsasitic, and cynical of Tess. They will still give out compliments to the player, but often the success will be self-attributed.

Think Bentham from One Piece.

Tess is still fairly positive, trying to make the best of this oddball professor.
