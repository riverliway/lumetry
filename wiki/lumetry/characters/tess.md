# Tess (she/her)

The PhD student the player controls from the first room to the last. Eager to please, hardworking, dedicated to a fault. She starts the run overflowing with first-year energy and gets ground down across the game, until the dialogue portraits show her running on fumes and spite.

In-game she is a simplified chibi model on the hex grid. In dialogue she gets the same fully rendered portrait treatment as the faculty, which is where her slow collapse actually plays out. See [art direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md).

## The name

A nickname of Tesla (unit of magnetism)

## Art

Tess is on the shorter side. Wears a white lab coat and has a clipboard. Has big, round, clear glasses.

### Sprites

**Excited** - Big, wide eyes looking slightly up. Open happy smile. Clutching her clipboard against her chest. White lab coat is pristine. Her hair is up in a tight bun.

**Singed** - She is holding up the bottom of her lab coat, there is a black burn mark on the bottom. She looks concerned/surprised.

**Fried** - Her hair is frizzy and one strand standing up is slightly on fire. The front of her face is darkened with soot and she looks shocked (eyes open wide). Her lab coat is also darkened too. Her glasses are also covered in soot and are falling off her face, the mark of where they were still left around her eyes.

**Victory-1** - Arms raised above head, eyes closed, very happy.

**Confused** - Holding her clipboard with both arms straight in the center of her hips. Questioning look on her face with a question mark next to her head.

**Grimacing** - Head turned away, left hand up and limp. Right hand clutching clipboard around navel. Grimacing expression.

**Facepalm** - Head slightly back, left hand palming her forehead. Wrinkled mouth. Right hand down by waist with clipboard hanging loosely.

**Bashful** - Eyes to the bottom left, right hand straight down holding the clipboard. Left hand holding onto her right elbow. Blushing.

**Sick-of-this-shit** - Both arms down at her sides. Face flattened. Wrinked mouth. Eyebags. Hair slightly unkempt.

## Role

She is the agent of player choice. Every mechanic in the game is something Tess does, pushing, spinning, toggling. See the [gameplay overview](#lumetry/gameplay/overview.md).
