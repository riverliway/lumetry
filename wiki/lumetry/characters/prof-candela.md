# Prof. Candela (she/they)

This trans-femme is moody and a little goth. You would guess the dark circles under her eyes are makeup. They are not. They are the insomnia. She introduces [beams with a finite reach](#lumetry/mechanics/beam-length.md). She is married to Prof. Lumen.

![Concept sketches of Prof. Candela](/lumetry/characters/prof-candela.png)

## Unit and shape

The candela measures luminous intensity, how strongly a source throws light in a single direction. Her mechanic caps how far a beam carries before it dies, so her rooms become about spending a limited reach well. The tired, low-wattage read suits a woman whose whole deal is light that only goes so far. Dark palette, per [art direction](#lumetry/art-direction.md).

## Art

Long, dark hair. The outside color is pitch black but the undercoat of the hair is a dark, midnight purple. Very pale (vitimin D decifincy skin), Their lab coat is fully buttoned up. Her lipstick is the same midnight purple. Her eyeshadow is a very dulled WLW flag.
