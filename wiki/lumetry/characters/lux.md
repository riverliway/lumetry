# Lux (they/them)

The intern. Timid, a walking ball of anxiety, silver-white hair over a young and childish face. Reads femboy without tipping into e-girl territory. Teaches no hardware. Lux is the last wing of the game: the intern [Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) has to teach, where you cooperate instead of do it all yourself.

## Unit and shape

The lux measures illuminance, light falling onto a surface per unit area.

## Art

Shorter than Tess, bangs covering their eyes. Lab coat a little too big for their arms, so it flops over their hands. 

## Dialogue

Unconfident.

Tess starts this wing annoyed at their incompetance, but slowly warms to them and is eventually proud of them by the end. Still exhausted.
