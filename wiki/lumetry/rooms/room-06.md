# room-06

Room 6 of 18, the final room in [Prof. Radian](#lumetry/characters/prof-radian.md)'s wing, on [rotation pads](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md).

## Puzzle

There is a greyed out rotation pad that can't be moved by hand. It gets rotated once the beam detector next to it lights up. The player needs to push the crate over, breaking the laser so they can get past it to turn on the laser which can move the rotation pad.

To softlock yourself:
* turn on both lasers, blocking one with a mirror and the other with the crate. then move into the softlock territory and push both back to unblock the lasers. this can be done on three sides of the middle
