# achievements

Six achievements, one per faculty [wing](#lumetry/gameplay/overview.md). The 18 [rooms](#lumetry/rooms/room-01.md) split six ways, three to a mentor, so every third room closes out a professor and pops their badge. The names ride the unit gags. The sixth doubles as the completion award.

## The six

### Push Comes to Shove
Clear [Room 3](#lumetry/rooms/room-03.md), the end of [Prof. Newton](#lumetry/characters/prof-newton.md)'s wing on [pushable mirrors](#lumetry/mechanics/pushable-mirrors.md). Three rooms in and you have the himbo's whole thesis: some problems really do move, if you lean on them hard enough.

### Pirouette
Clear [Room 6](#lumetry/rooms/room-06.md), the close of [Prof. Radian](#lumetry/characters/prof-radian.md)'s wing on [rotation pads](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md). Every block turned to the one angle it needed. Radian calls that choreography and expects a bow.

### Burning the Candle
Clear [Room 9](#lumetry/rooms/room-09.md), the end of [Prof. Candela](#lumetry/characters/prof-candela.md)'s wing on [finite beam reach](#lumetry/mechanics/beam-length.md). Not a photon of reach wasted. You and Candela are both running on the last of yours.

### Full Spectrum
Clear [Room 12](#lumetry/rooms/room-12.md), the close of [Prof. Lumen](#lumetry/characters/prof-lumen.md)'s wing on [prisms and color](#lumetry/mechanics/prisms.md). One white beam split into every color, and every color sent where it belonged. Glitter not included, but implied.

### Melting Point
Clear [Room 15](#lumetry/rooms/room-15.md), the end of [Prof. Joule](#lumetry/characters/prof-joule.md)'s wing on the [beam focuser](#lumetry/mechanics/beam-focuser.md). Three beams into one throat, and something in the room stopped existing. Joule has never been happier.

### Passing the Torch
Clear [Room 18](#lumetry/rooms/room-18.md), the finale, where [Lux](#lumetry/characters/lux.md) is the intern and you are finally the one teaching (see [cooperation](#lumetry/mechanics/cooperation.md)). You started the run as the student without a unit. You end it a Tesla, showing someone else which way the light bends. The completion badge.

### Crisped
Get fried in every room.

### Boxed In
Softlock yourself in every room

## Presentation

Each badge is the mentor's [portrait](#lumetry/art-direction.md), so a full shelf reads as the faculty lined up in order, Newton to Lux, a class photo assembled one wing at a time. Cheap to make, since the portraits already exist for dialogue and box-art.
