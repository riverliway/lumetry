# overview

Lumetry is a room-at-a-time puzzle game. Each room is one experiment: a fixed hex-grid chamber holding a [laser emitter](#lumetry/mechanics/laser-emitters.md), some hardware to manipulate, and one or more [measurement devices](#lumetry/mechanics/detectors.md). Light the devices, the door unlocks, move on.

## The loop

1. A professor's portrait opens the room and introduces the new idea.
2. The player solves the single puzzle, using everything the earlier rooms taught plus the new piece.
3. Faculty banter plays between chambers.
4. Next room.

Rooms stay short and self-contained on purpose. The whole run targets ~60 minutes across 18 experiments, six wings of three, one wing per faculty member, each layering one concept onto familiar ones. What opens as plain reflection becomes multi-step, multi-color optics by the end. Every third room closes a wing and pops an [achievement](#lumetry/gameplay/achievements.md).

## Verbs

[Tess](#lumetry/characters/tess.md) moves on the six-way hex grid, faces a direction, and *uses* whatever she is facing. That is the whole vocabulary: walk, push a mirror along its [track](#lumetry/mechanics/pushable-mirrors.md), spin a block on a [rotation pad](#lumetry/mechanics/rotation-pads.md), toggle an [emitter](#lumetry/mechanics/laser-emitters.md). Live beams are solid to her; she cannot walk through her own light, which quietly makes the beam a piece of the puzzle geometry.

## The arc

Tess starts the run wired and eager and ends it visibly wrecked, the portraits tracking her decline. The faculty escalate their hardware wing by wing until the last one flips the roles: Tess, taught in every room until now, becomes the teacher, guiding [Lux](#lumetry/characters/lux.md) the intern through a [cooperation](#lumetry/mechanics/cooperation.md) finale that cashes in every mechanic at once. She earns her unit at the end (Tess, short for Tesla) by graduating from student to mentor.

## Release

Free on Steam. Optional paid extras (art book, soundtrack) for supporters. Marketing kept small, a few posts on modest social accounts.
